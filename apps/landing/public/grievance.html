How to raise a complaint

  1. Problem with an order? Open the order in the app and tap Get help with this under that shop. The court manager sees it at once.
  2. Anything else, or not sorted? Email the Grievance Officer with your name, the email or phone on your account, your order number (if any) and what went wrong.

Our timelines: we acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours with a reference number, and resolve order complaints within 1 month. Complaints about your personal data are answered within 90 days at the latest, usually much sooner.

If you are not satisfied, you can contact the National Consumer Helpline (dial 1915) about orders, or the Data Protection Board of India about your data.
