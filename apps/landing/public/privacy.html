1. The short version

  • You can order at a table as a guest. We then keep no name, email or phone unless you choose to add them.
  • An account holds your name, email, mobile number and your orders. You can download it or delete it from Account → Privacy and account.
  • Payments go through Razorpay. We never see your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • We use no advertising or analytics trackers, and we never sell your data.
  • Offers and announcements are off unless you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time.

4. Your consent and how to withdraw it

  • Ordering as a guest needs no account. We use the order details only to make and hand over your food and keep the accounts.
  • Creating an account: you tick a box agreeing to this policy and the Terms. The box is never ticked for you. We record the version you agreed to, and when.
  • Offers and announcements are a separate choice, off by default. Turn them on or off any time in Account → Notifications. We record each change.
  • Notifications on this phone are turned on or off in the same place, or in your browser settings.
  • Withdraw everything by deleting your account (Account → Privacy and account → Delete my account), or by emailing our Grievance Officer. Withdrawing doesn't undo what we did lawfully before, and we still keep the order records the law requires.

5. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • Get a copy of your data: Account → Privacy and account → Download my data. Email us for a summary of how it was used and who we shared it with.
  • Correct or update it: Account → Profile and preferences, or email us.
  • Erase it: delete your account in the app, or email us.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act, by emailing us.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer. We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 30 days; data complaints are always answered within 90 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We use no advertising, analytics or social media cookies or pixels. The only things we store in your browser are needed for the service to work:

  • Sign-in (Google Firebase, in browser storage): keeps you signed in, as a guest or with your account.
  • Cart, table session and order progress (local and session storage): so your cart and live order survive a page refresh.
  • Menu cache: a copy of the menus, so the app loads fast on weak Wi-Fi.
  • Payment window (Razorpay): their checkout may set cookies it needs to take and secure your payment. See Razorpay's privacy policy.

Because these are strictly necessary, we don't show a cookie banner. You can clear them any time in your browser settings; you will be signed out and lose your cart. If we ever add optional trackers, we will ask you first, with "Accept" and "Reject" equally easy, and add a "Cookie settings" link to every page. Full details are in our Cookie Policy.

7. Children

Qrave is a food-ordering service and we do not ask for anyone's age. We do not track, profile or send targeted offers to anyone, and never knowingly to children, and we do not show advertising. If you believe a child's personal data has been given to us and want it removed, email our Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

8. How we keep it safe

  • Every Qrave site runs only on HTTPS.
  • Database access is denied by default. You can read only your own data; kitchens see only their own tickets; only the court manager sees customer lists. Only our servers can create orders or payments.
  • Prices, payments and refunds are checked on our servers, never trusted from the phone. Payment confirmations from Razorpay are signature-checked.
  • Card and UPI details are handled only by Razorpay, which is PCI-DSS certified. Payment keys are kept in Google Secret Manager, never in the app.
  • Staff and admin actions are written to an audit log.
  • We have a written plan for data breaches. If one affects you, we will tell you without delay and report it to the Data Protection Board of India (with a full report within 72 hours) and cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours, as the law requires.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this page and its date, and ask for your agreement again in the app before the change applies to you.
