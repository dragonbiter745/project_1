Cancellations and Refunds

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Qrave orders are freshly cooked food, collected at the shop counter or brought to your table. Nothing is shipped to an address, so there are no returns, exchanges, warranties or return shipping charges. Here is when you get your money back.

You get a full refund, automatically, when

  • A shop rejects your order (for example, a dish ran out). You get back that shop's part of the order.
  • A shop does not accept your order in time. If a shop hasn't accepted within a few minutes, its part is cancelled and refunded.
  • A payment fails or times out after money left your account. Any amount taken goes back to the same account.
  • You were charged twice for the same order. The extra payment is refunded.

Each shop in a combined order is handled separately: if one shop rejects, only its part is refunded, and the other shops still make your food.

Cancelling an order yourself

  • Before the shop starts cooking: ask at that shop's counter. If they cancel it, you get a full refund for that shop's part.
  • After the shop starts cooking: the order can no longer be cancelled, because the food is being made for you.

Wrong, missing or bad food

Tell the shop counter straight away, or open the order in the app and tap Get help with this under that shop. The court manager sees it at once. If the shop or the court manager finds something went wrong, you get a full or partial refund for the affected items, or a fresh dish.

How long a refund takes

  • The refund starts straight away for automatic refunds, and as soon as a manual refund is approved.
  • It goes back to the same UPI ID, card or account you paid from. We cannot refund in cash or to a different account.
  • Banks usually show it in 5 to 7 working days. You can follow it under Account → Payments and refunds.
  • If it hasn't arrived after 7 working days, contact us with your order number and we will chase it with the payment gateway.

A refund returns the amount you actually paid. Coupon discounts and points are not paid out as cash. Points are only earned on food you collected, so refunded orders earn no points.

Paying at the counter

If you paid the shop directly at the counter (cash or the shop's own UPI), the shop refunds you directly.

Prices and charges

Before you pay, the cart shows every charge: each dish, GST, any platform fee, any discount, and the final total. Nothing is added after you pay. We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets through Razorpay Payments.
