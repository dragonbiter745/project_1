1. What Qrave does

Qrave is an ordering platform for the food court at REVA University. You scan the QR code on a table (or use the website for pickup), order from one or more shops in a single cart, pay once, and collect your food with a token from each shop.

The food is sold and prepared by the individual shops, not by Qrave. Each shop is responsible for its food, ingredients, allergen information, hygiene and food safety licence. Qrave takes your order, collects payment on the shops' behalf and passes the order to them. The shops on Qrave are listed on the Grievance and contact page.
