Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Qrave uses no advertising, analytics, social media or tracking cookies or pixels, on the home page or in the ordering app. We only store what the app needs to work.

Do we ask for consent?

All of the above is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for, so we don't show a cookie banner. Nothing above is used to track you across sites, build a profile or show ads.

If we ever add optional cookies (for example, analytics), they will load only after you agree. You will get a banner with Accept, Reject and Choose buttons that are equally easy to use, and a "Cookie settings" link on every page to change your mind.

How to clear them

You can clear Qrave's data in your browser settings (Site settings → order.getqrave.in → Clear data). You will be signed out and your cart will be emptied. Your account and past orders stay safe on our servers.
